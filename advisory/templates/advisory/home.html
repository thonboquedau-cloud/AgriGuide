About AgriGuide

AgriGuide is a mobile-based farmer advisory system designed to provide smallholder farmers with accessible and practical agricultural information. The system helps farmers access crop production advice, livestock information, farming tips, weather updates, farming calendars, and information about common pests and diseases.
